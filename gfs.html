Greenwich Finance Series

Finance & AI Workshop · Greenwich Town Hall · 2026

Overview

Co-organized and led the AI section of the second Greenwich Finance Series event — a student-run financial literacy initiative at Greenwich Town Hall. 40+ students from 9 area high schools attended.

What I Did

  • Built a live AI stock prediction agent and presented it to 40+ high school students.
  • Led a discussion on how AI agents work and how they are being used in financial markets today.
  • Walked attendees through the code and natural language processing behind the agent.
  • Students built their own version of the agent hands-on during the session.

The Event

  • The evening opened with finance professionals covering income, taxes, budgeting, and investing fundamentals.
  • Speakers included Jon Wendell (Nassau Point Investors, Harvard Business School), Mack Portnoy (VP, J.P. Morgan Private Bank), and Saphora Afkhami (J.P. Morgan Private Bank).
  • Schools represented: Greenwich High School, Brunswick, Greenwich Academy, GCDS, Sacred Heart, Stamford High School, King School, Westhill, and Windrose.

Quanta Bricks

Research Intern · Benchmarking and outreach for ChemOrchestra, a computational chemistry platform

Overview

Quanta Bricks builds ChemOrchestra, an AI platform for computational drug design. I work with the team on two fronts: benchmarking the platform's model outputs against real experimental data, and leading student outreach to bring the tool into a high school research setting.

Benchmarking

  • Evaluate ChemOrchestra's model outputs against published experimental data rather than other computational methods — a stronger, data-quality-focused test of the platform.
  • Proposed PETase as a benchmark case, since the literature offers strong experimental anchors (melting-temperature measurements, published crystal structures, and catalytic data) to compare model predictions against.

Student Outreach

  • Leading a pilot to introduce ChemOrchestra as a tool for independent student research at my school.
  • Authored a one-pager framing the pilot for school administrators and teachers.
  • TAing a class where I introduce the platform so students can use it hands-on, modeled on Quanta Bricks' own research course.
  • Helped plan Quanta Bricks' first bio-hackathon.
